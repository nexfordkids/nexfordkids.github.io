About Nexford Kids

Because ordinary was never the plan

Building Bright Futures, One Preschool at a Time.

Nexford Kids is a modern international preschool brand dedicated to providing joyful, child-centered early education inspired by global best practices. Our exclusive Limited Curriculum combines experiential learning, creativity, life skills, and strong academic foundations to help every child learn with confidence and curiosity.

We believe that every child deserves a safe, nurturing, and inspiring environment where learning happens through exploration, imagination, and meaningful experiences.

Together, we nurture young minds and build a stronger tomorrow.

Our Vision

To become one of India's most trusted international preschool brands by nurturing confident, creative, and future-ready learners while empowering passionate entrepreneurs to build successful preschool businesses across the country.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality early childhood education through innovative teaching methods, world-class curriculum, continuous teacher development, and comprehensive franchise support that creates lasting value for children, parents, and franchise partners.

ABOUT NEXFORD KIDS

More Than a Preschool. A Complete Early-Learning Ecosystem.

NEXFORD Kids International Preschool is built on a simple but powerful belief: The early years should not simply prepare a child for the next class. They should prepare the child for life.

NEXFORD is a modern early-childhood education brand created to bring together meaningful learning, child development, life skills, creativity, technology, structured academics and real-world experiences within one carefully designed preschool ecosystem.

With 30+ years of combined educational expertise, NEXFORD brings together experience and a forward-looking approach to early childhood education. Our vision is not merely to create another preschool, but to develop an educational environment where every child is encouraged to explore, communicate, question, create, participate and grow with confidence.

Our Philosophy

At NEXFORD, we believe that every child is different.

Children do not learn at exactly the same pace, in exactly the same way or with exactly the same interests. Therefore, our approach looks beyond simply completing a syllabus.

We focus on understanding the child behind the marks.

What does the child enjoy?

Where is the child developing confidently?

Where does the child need additional support?

What skills are emerging?

What should the next learning goal be?

These questions form an important part of the NEXFORD approach.

At the heart of NEXFORD is our LUMINED Curriculum, specially designed by the NEXFORD academic team for the early years.

Not just learning what to think — but developing the confidence, curiosity and skills to explore how to think.

At NEXFORD, learning extends into activities, conversations, experiments, creative expression, movement, problem-solving, social interaction and real-world experiences.

With 70+ brain-boosting activities throughout the year, children are given regular opportunities to learn by doing, exploring and participating.

This helps transform:

Concept → Experience

Experience → Understanding

Understanding → Skill

Because children shouldn’t merely remember what they were taught.

They should experience it.
