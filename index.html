FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Have Questions? We've Got Answers

Here are answers to some of the most common questions parents ask before admission.

What is the minimum age for admission?

+

Children can join Play Group from the age of 2 years. Age requirements vary for Nursery, LKG and UKG.

Is transport available?

+

Yes. Safe transport with trained staff is available in selected locations.

Do you provide meals?

+

We encourage healthy eating habits and provide nutritious meal options where applicable.

Can parents visit the campus?

+

Absolutely. We encourage parents to schedule a school tour before admission.
